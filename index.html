MSc Thesis — Survival Analysis

Time-to-event modelling of dementia and Alzheimer's disease in ~4,000 twin pairs

Cox proportional hazards models and cumulative incidence functions applied to longitudinal registry data from the Danish Twin Registry, studying genetic influences on lifespan variation in relation to neurodegenerative disease.

Cox PH CIF ACE models R Stata
MSc Thesis — Genetic Analysis

GWAS pipeline: 80M → 4M variants after QC across ~1,500 individuals

Built a reproducible genome-wide association pipeline in PLINK and Bash. Identified 4–5 genome-wide significant SNPs for Alzheimer's disease and suggestive loci for dementia. Cross-trait analysis via Genomic SEM and HDL.

PLINK Bash GRM Genomic SEM HDL
Individual Study Activity

Heritability estimation — survival frameworks on simulated twin data

Developed time-to-event frameworks with explicit handling of censoring and dependent observations. Estimated variance components using ACE/ADE models with model comparison via AIC.

survival mets prodlim R
BSc Thesis

SNP–osteoporosis associations in haematological malignancy patients

Tested candidate genetic associations in a clinical cancer cohort. No significant associations detected — the null result drove critical evaluation of study power, clinical confounders, and the limitations of candidate-gene designs.
